## Hero generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: ultrawide cinematic hero landscape artwork for a sophisticated personal life advisory website called The Diplomat Within. Create one original landscape image, wide 3:2 or wider composition, without any text or logos. Hawaiʻi-inspired volcanic coast and Pacific ocean at warm dawn: deep blue-green sea, white surf tracing a curving dark rocky shore, lush organic green headland entering from the right, distant misty mountain silhouettes, warm ivory pale gold sky and softly illuminated clouds. Expansive, inspirational, alive, peaceful but with the energy of possibility. Beautiful editorial travel imagery with atmospheric photographic realism and a subtly painterly fine-art finish, rich natural texture, not flat vector art. Horizon around upper third. LEFT HALF predominantly dark deep ocean water and soft shadow, uncluttered to support large ivory heading overlay; visually interesting sunlight and dramatic landforms concentrated right half. Natural muted tones ocean #193C4A, forest, sage, warm sand, faint terracotta dawn. No people, no houses, no tourist clichés, no text, no watermark, no oversaturated orange, no fantasy effects. Do not copy any existing photograph or depict a specific real location exactly. This is original island-inspired conceptual landscape art, not documentary photography.

## Garden generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an original high-end editorial landscape artwork for a warm personal life advisory website. Wide 3:2 image: a quiet narrow path through a lush Hawaiʻi-inspired tropical garden, gently curving from foreground left toward a glimpse of open blue ocean in upper right, dappled early morning sunlight through graceful leaves, rich moss and fern texture, naturally weathered stone, warm earthy sand and forest greens. Inviting and hopeful, a tangible sense of making room for life and possibilities, refined cinematic travel-journal feeling, subtly painterly atmospheric photographic realism. Focus on organic greenery and beautiful light, no people, no buildings, no words, no logos, no watermark. No generic wellness symbols, no lotus, no saturated neon greens, no fantasy. This is conceptual generated scenery, not a claim of a specific real location. Composition should work as a large cropped portrait insert as well as wide background.
